# Portable wall master · correction on PR #52

Author checks pass. **Independent Art QA and owner wall-master approval remain pending.** This correction adds a portable model; it does not expand the asset library or alter any accepted sprite, source geometry, camera or production code.

The GLB contains one identity root and one wall mesh with 13 PBR material primitives, 11,824 triangles and two embedded 1024² PNG atlases. All 48 source wall components are represented. The original `.blend` remains editable and byte-identical to accepted commit `8b2aa6e406adec28ddd21c6cf8caabe6c93ac609`, as do `render.py` and the six reviewed PNG exports. Ground catcher, studio lights/camera, background, animation and directional lighting are excluded from the GLB. The dark blocked-cell footing remains part of wall geometry.

Native glTF bounds are X/Z ±.5 and Y 0… .595. In Blender the importer reverses the documented `(X,Y,Z) → (X,Z,−Y)` basis conversion, restoring +Z up with X/Y ±.5 and Z 0… .595. Both root and mesh have identity transforms and a ground-center origin. One glTF metre equals one logical cell; forward/increasing row is native glTF +Z / Blender −Y. Sprite elevation belongs to QA cameras, not model geometry.

`glb-reimport.json` records the binary, UV/PBR and clean import checks. Blender was factory-reset to zero objects/images, then loaded a GLB-only copy from a temporary directory. Before adding QA lookdev it contained exactly EMPTY + MESH, two packed images and no lights/cameras. The mesh preserves triangle count, surface area and signed volume, with **maximum two-way source-position error 5.96046448×10⁻⁸ cell** and **minimum source/import corner-normal dot 0.99999724**. A downward ray through the open top hits the recessed floor at z=.094 with an upward normal. Exported normals/tangents are finite, unit length and orthogonal within numerical tolerance; UVs are finite and bounded. Embedded PNG bytes match the separately supplied texture checksums. All original roughness/metallic material values survive.

Joining nonuniformly scaled components initially drifted some weighted normals. The exporter now explicitly restores evaluated source corner normals before baking/export. Blender's four-decimal tangent output is normalized and orthogonalized in the binary payload afterward, without altering positions, UVs, normals or tangent handedness. Those steps are repeatable in `export-glb.py` and checked after import.

Base color is baked through a temporary emission surface connected directly to original Principled Base Color; no studio lighting, cast shadows, AO or AgX display grade enter the atlas. A tangent-space normal bake preserves the procedural grain/bump as a texture. The source components' local Generated-coordinate fields are retained through the temporary join so grain is not stretched over the assembled wall. Roughness and metallic remain scalar PBR parameters per original material, including iron.

**Representation differences remain explicit:** finite 1024², 8-bit textures approximate continuous procedural grain and bump; small surface details can filter differently at close range. Editable nodes/modifiers remain in `.blend`. Core glTF metallic/roughness PBR cannot carry Blender-specific shader implementation/specular-IOR details, and other render engines may differ. No high-detail or weathering variant was created. This 11.8k-triangle interchange master can be instanced as a whole; a later runtime LOD/material consolidation needs separate authorization and the same footprint/normal/readability checks.

`glb-render-checks.json` measures original/source and clean-import renders from the official 58°/yaw-0 camera in both moods. **Silhouette support differs by zero pixels.** RGB8 mean absolute differences over the visible body are **2.046 daylight / 1.498 dusk**, with 95th-percentile per-channel differences **7 / 5**. Maximum individual channel differences **109 / 54** are also retained in the report rather than claiming pixel identity. This is a source/import comparison, not a new sprite approval. The QA rig recreates the actual source World Background node (linear RGB .0508760884, strength 1); the existing pilot's `world.color` metadata alone does not describe that shader. Source lighting/render scripts remain untouched.

Author visually inspected source/import daylight/dusk renders and the native contact comparison. The opening, inset floor, corner caps and bolts, slat seams, footing and warm/cool material treatment remain consistent. No missing texture, flipped face, new opening or displaced component was observed. The original mobile/adjacency screenshots and their previously documented terrain seams remain unchanged. This does not supply independent QA, physical-device performance or owner signoff.

The additional `glb-repeat-export.json` check re-exported the model and both atlases and verified **byte-identical outputs on Blender 4.3.2 in this environment**. This does not promise byte identity across platforms or Blender/PNG encoder versions.
