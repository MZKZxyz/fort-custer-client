Move. Stop. Compare.

  1. In A, hold Right to pick up X and clear F. You stay beside the cleared tile.
  2. Release. Press Right again to enter, then reach the tipi. Reset and try B.

04 / Working recommendation

Lead with the D-pad.

Its visible directions and immediate first step make stopping and re-pressing explicit. Keep drag as an optional alternative for players who prefer steering from one contact.

A design hypothesis, pending physical-device playtests and independent QA. This schematic does not evaluate final art or ranked visibility.
